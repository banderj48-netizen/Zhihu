数字分身的设计理念来自于酒馆，一种专门用于角色扮演的提示词工程。分身人格存放在一张结构化的角色卡中，每次发言前按固定顺序组装成 prompt，交给模型生成回应。

The design concept of a digital avatar comes from taverns, a kind of prompt engineering specifically for role-playing. The avatar’s personality is stored in a structured character card, and before each line is spoken, it’s assembled into a prompt in a fixed order.
